# Color Replacement Guide

Use Find & Replace in your code editor to update all colors across the app:

## Color Replacements

### Primary Colors (Brown/Orange → Soft Pink)
- Find: `#C87D3D` Replace with: `#FF8B9E`
- Find: `#D4A574` Replace with: `#FFC4D0`

### Secondary Colors (Teal → Soft Lavender)  
- Find: `#5B9A96` Replace with: `#B8A8D8`

### Background Colors (Warm Cream → Soft Purple/White)
- Find: `#FFFBF5` Replace with: `#FAF7FF`
- Find: `#F5EDE3` Replace with: `#F3EFFF`
- Find: `#FEFAF3` Replace with: `#FEFBFF`

### Text Colors (Dark Brown → Soft Purple/Gray)
- Find: `#3D2817` Replace with: `#4A4458`
- Find: `#8B7355` Replace with: `#8B7E9E`

### Dark Backgrounds (Brown → Soft Purple Gradient)
- Find: `from-[#3D2817] via-[#5B3A1F] to-[#3D2817]` 
  Replace with: `from-[#E8DEFF] via-[#F5F0FF] to-[#FFE8F0]`

### Additional Accent Colors
- Find: `#F4A261` Replace with: `#FFB5A7` (peachy soft)
- Find: `#9B9ECE` Replace with: `#B8A8D8` (soft lavender)

After replacing, your app will have the soft, calming pastel aesthetic!
